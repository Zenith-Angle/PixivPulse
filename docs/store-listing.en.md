# PixivPulse - Creator Growth Tracker

## Short description

Track growth across your Pixiv works with local snapshots, trends, conversion metrics, and optional AI analysis.

## Detailed description

PixivPulse helps Pixiv creators turn individual observations into a history of how their works perform.

Sign in to Pixiv in the same browser to record available metrics from work management, including views, bookmarks, likes, and comments. Explore cumulative trends, interval changes, and conversion metrics in a local dashboard. Choose English, Simplified Chinese, or your browser's language.

FEATURES

• Portfolio overview: review totals, recent changes, follower observations, and ranking information where available.
• Work library: search by title, ID, or series, filter content types, and inspect individual work histories.
• Work comparisons: compare 2–5 works on a shared timeline using cumulative views, bookmarks, and likes or interval changes.
• Flexible ranges: explore today, the last 24 hours, 3, 7, or 30 days, 3 months, all observations, or a custom range.
• Ongoing tracking: sync manually or on a schedule. Scheduled runs follow fixed Beijing-time slots; manual runs do not shift the schedule.
• On-page growth indicators: see today's metric changes on Pixiv's work management page.
• Backup and restore: export JSON or CSV, preview and validate imports, and manage local storage.
• Optional AI analysis: connect your own API endpoint, model, and key to discuss your work. With local data sharing enabled, the Agent can query relevant statistics. Sampling novel text requires a separate opt-in.

LOCAL DATA AND LIMITATIONS

Core charts and statistics work without an API key. Work history and settings are stored locally in your browser. If you enable AI data sharing, relevant query results are sent to your configured provider. Enabling original-text sampling may also send excerpts from selected novels. Review your provider's policies before enabling these options.

The first sync establishes a baseline. PixivPulse cannot recover history from before installation or reconstruct hourly changes during gaps in collection. It does not collect while your browser is closed or your device is asleep. Missing observations are distinguished from measured zero growth. Metrics do not reveal traffic sources and do not guarantee creative growth.

PixivPulse is an independent tool, not an official Pixiv product, and is not affiliated with Pixiv. Requires Chrome 120 or later and an accessible signed-in Pixiv session.
